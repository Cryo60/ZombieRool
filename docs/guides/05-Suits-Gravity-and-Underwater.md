# Suits, gravity and underwater maps

## Suit stations

Place a Lunar Helmet/Astronaut, Diving, or Hazmat station. Leave room for its two-block height. In a match, interact with either half to obtain the matching equipment.

**J** equips/removes it. The outfit and first-person arms change. The original lunar helmet remains available. All three appearances share environmental protection; hazmat does not introduce a separate poison-resistance mechanic.

A suit and portable device can be carried together. Portable equipment uses **H**.

For an equipped start, use **Map Config → Player → Loadout → Suit equipped at match start**: None, Astronaut, Diving or Hazmat. Save before starting.

## An airless Moon area

1. In **Environment → Atmosphere & breathing**, configure reduced gravity and vacuum.
2. Provide suit access or start players equipped.
3. Put an **Atmosphere Zone** around an air-filled room and set vacuum off, gravity 1.
4. Test entering/leaving, removing the suit, dying and restarting.

Atmosphere zones control physical environmental rules. Sky presets control presentation. A Moon sky alone does not make an area airless; a teleporter arrival preset does not replace gravity/oxygen configuration.

## Underwater maps

Actual water and real air pockets are recognized automatically. Keep gravity normal and vacuum off for ordinary underwater areas. Oxygen drains when the player's head is immersed. The ZombieRool gauge replaces vanilla drowning bubbles; an equipped environmental suit protects underwater.

Build a sealed room containing actual air for an air pocket. A zone cannot turn water into air. Water Breathing and Conduit Power protect in water, but not vacuum.

Enemy navigation still needs valid routes. Zombies follow the bottom and steps; a vertical wall does not automatically become a swimming route.

**Before sharing:** check oxygen recovery, station access, suit visibility, gravity transitions and a survivable route for unequipped players.
