# Portable equipment and Warden Locks

## Placement and retrieval

Acquire equipment from a workbench or reward. **H** holds/stows it. Only one portable device can be carried or deployed per player. Suits use a separate slot and **J**.

Hold a turbine or sentry turret, aim at supported ground and confirm its preview with left/right click. Look at your own device within three blocks and press F or right-click to retrieve it. Wear is preserved. Path Blocks are supported and restored when the device is removed. Leave clear space for its full model.

## Zombie Shield

Stowed, it protects your back; held, your front. Attack/melee bashes. H stows it again. You cannot fire a gun while holding it. Shift + right-click deploys it on a surface.

Blocked hits and successful bashes cause wear. Impacts produce shake and sound. If it breaks, return to the completed workbench recipe for another when copies remain available.

## Turbine

Startup takes **2.5 seconds**: orange means starting; green, sound and particles confirm power. Nearby compatible machines receive local power, blocked by solid walls. Global mode still requires global power; Both requires both.

The turbine operates ordinary doors, but **does not open/pay for Buyable Doors**. Retrieval leaves power for **1.5 seconds**. Destruction cuts it immediately. Enemies can attack it; retrieving/replacing it does not repair its wear.

## Sentry Turret

It needs no external power and shoots visible enemies with fixed damage. Players can walk through it. Enemies must really strike it to cause wear. Damage produces shake, a red flash and sparks. When destroyed, it stops firing, lowers its head and disappears. It cannot be retrieved during destruction.

## Warden Lock setup

1. Attach a lock to a normal door or Buyable Door. Properly paired double doors are locked together.
2. In **Map Config → Bosses → Loot**, choose eligible key-dropping bosses. Brutus has it on by default.
3. During play, kill an eligible boss, look at its dropped key and press F.
4. Everyone gains access, including late arrivals. Death does not remove ownership; Tab shows it.
5. Look at a lock and press F. It falls away. Interact again to open or buy the door; unlocking does not pay the price.

An uncollected key can despawn and drop again from another eligible boss while still needed. A new match restores locks and clears the team's key.
