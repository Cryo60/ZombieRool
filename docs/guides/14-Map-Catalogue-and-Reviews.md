# Map catalogue and reviews

The map menu opens on **Official**. Browse thumbnail cards, search and filter maps, or open the community catalogue. Automatic featured selections help surface maps from catalogue activity and ratings; they are not hand-picked endorsements.

Open a card for framed map information: author, description, game and map versions, compatibility, dependencies, installation state and reviews. An **Outdated** badge means the map targets an older ZombieRool version. Check its requirements before playing; the badge alone does not prove the map is broken. Dependency names show their full links on hover and can open the relevant page.

**Get a link** shows a selectable download address and a **Copy link** button. The in-game catalogue does not install it. Follow the map's instructions or use the separate ZombieRool launcher, then check that the world and required mods are installed in the intended instance.

## Rate a map from Minecraft

Choose one to five stars on its detail page, optionally write a comment, then publish. Reviews use your verified Minecraft account: each account has one editable rating per map. Submitting another rating updates that account's review. It does not use your Discord identity.

Publishing requires an authenticated Minecraft session, a connection and a compatible community service. If the screen says the reviews service needs an update, existing reviews remain readable but new submissions are unavailable until that service is updated. Offline catalogue information may be cached.

## Publish a map

Map publication remains on the community website through Discord sign-in. Minecraft reviews do not create a map listing or replace that publication account. Provide accurate versions, dependencies, screenshots and installation instructions when publishing.
