# Wonder weapons and combat

The Ray Gun has revised firing recoil, luminous projectile trails and impact effects. Close-range shots can cause self-damage; leave space between yourself and the target. The weapon's recoil recovers between shots.

The Ray Gun Mark II uses a three-shot burst with a recoil impulse for each shot and revised energy effects. Its TaCZ integration supports penetrating shots without explosive or incendiary impacts. Firing does not push the player's body backwards.

The TaCZ Wunderwaffe uses ZombieRool's electric chain and stun behavior instead of explosive impacts. Its identity comes from linking enemies with electricity.

The Whisper's final shot releases crows from the victim's head. Its looping audio follows the player spatially. **The Collector** is the English name of the Percepteur.

TaCZ weapon behavior requires the corresponding compatible gun pack. Map makers should test both normal and Pack-a-Punched versions with their chosen pack and balance settings.
