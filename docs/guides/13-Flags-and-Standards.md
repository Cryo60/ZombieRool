# Flags and wall standards

Find them in the creative Decorative Props category by searching the country/faction name, Flag or Standard (French: drapeau / étendard).

Each pattern has a three-block-high pole flag (two cells wide) and a two-block-high wall standard (one cell wide). Place one item to reserve the complete footprint; breaking any part removes the full prop. Placement faces the player, or the clicked wall for standards. Leave the reserved cells clear. The cloth has baked folds rather than a ticking animation. No entity, particles or game-asset extraction is required.

Patterns: France, United Kingdom, Soviet Union, Japan, Imperial Japan (Rising Sun), United States, modern Russia, modern Germany, Cuba, historical Germany (the user-provided non-swastika cross/medallion design), Lordaeron, Scourge, Stormwind, Orgrimmar, Burning Legion, Scarlet Crusade and Forsaken. 17 patterns / 34 props.

Warcraft heraldry directly reuses the existing camos: lordaeron, scourge, alliance (Stormwind), horde (Orgrimmar), burning_legion, scarlet_crusade and forsaken. No substitute emblems are drawn. National reference images and provenance are in assets-source/26.7/flags; the historical German pattern follows the supplied reference. These are decorative objects and do not change faction/gameplay rules.

The ordinary Buyable Obstacle model-copy workflow applies. Provide the required footprint in obstacle blocks. A resource pack can replace `textures/block/flags/<id>.png`.

Both visible cloth faces retain their correct orientation across placement directions. Allow the whole reserved footprint when using these props in a Buyable Obstacle.
